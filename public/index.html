Scan a clothing tag, read the fabric.

Role Ideation to release
Team Five people, fully lateral
Stack Swift · SwiftUI · LLM
Status Published and maintained
Screens
Problem

In a store, the tag already tells you the composition and almost nobody can read it.

Audience

People who want a better cost-benefit on what they buy.

The angle is an informed purchase, not sustainability and not allergies.

My role

A team of five with no hierarchy between us.

I was in all of it: ideation, research, development, deploy, and launch.

An LLM reads the composition against a limited base of known answers.

A second pass checks for hallucinations before the user sees anything.

Sleevee

Scan, review, and organize your cards with a premium experience built for collectors.

Role Research, design, and all the code
Team Two, equal weight
Stack Swift · SwiftUI · Vision
Status In review for the App Store
Screens
Why it exists

It started in market research: one keyword with heavy search and little competition.

Understanding who was searching for it opened up trading card games as a whole.

An emerging market with real money moving through it, so we bet on it.

Audience

The lifestyle collector, with women on TikTok at the centre of it.

Collectr, HoloDex, Manabox and TCGBinder put data and price first.

This one puts the collection on display.

My role

Built in a pair, equal weight in research, design, and strategy.

The code was mine.

Technical decision

Written on the newest SwiftUI.

The card scanner runs fully offline through the Vision framework, while the rest of the category sends the image to a server.

Anjo S-Track

Safe speed before every curve, offline.

Role Took it from zero
Team Alone, first internship
Stack React Native · Android · BLE
Built in Six months
Screens
The product

A road-safety system that keeps trucks from rolling over in curves.

Hardware in the cab reads the road, the vehicle, and the load, and the app tells the driver the maximum safe speed before each curve.

Sold by subscription to fleets, device included.

My role

My first internship as a developer.

I took the project alone, from zero, and shipped it in six months.

Technical decision

The device sends road, vehicle, and load data to the phone over BLE, so the app works with no connection at all.

The interface is built for a moving truck: big bars, big numbers, and as little navigation as possible.

Why it is here

Everything else here is my own product, on iOS and SwiftUI.

This one is cross-platform, talks to hardware, and carries real weight: preventing accidents, not selling a consumer app.

Status

I no longer work at the company.

The app is still in use by the client fleets.

Where to find it

Not on a public store. It ships with the device, to fleets.
